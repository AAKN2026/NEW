Frequently asked questions

Everything parents need to know before requesting a tutor.

How does tutor matching work?

Tell us the student's level, subject, preferred lesson format, budget and requirements. We then use those details to guide the tutor options we recommend.

Do you offer home tuition?

Yes. You can request home tuition, online lessons, or either option when you submit your enquiry.

Can I request a specific tutor?

Yes. If you already have a tutor in mind, mention the tutor's name in your WhatsApp message and we'll help with the enquiry.

What happens after I submit?

Your request opens WhatsApp with the information you entered. Send the message to our team and we can continue the conversation there.

Can I change tutors?

If the fit isn't right, contact us and explain what needs to change. We'll help you discuss the next suitable option.

How quickly will I hear back?

Response time depends on enquiry volume and tutor availability. WhatsApp is the fastest way to start the conversation.
