Privacy Notice

Tutor Match collects information submitted through parent enquiries and tutor applications so that we can respond, assess suitability and facilitate tutor matching.

Information we may collect

Name, WhatsApp number, student level, subject, lesson requirements, location, schedule, budget and tutor profile information.

How we use it

We use submitted information for enquiry handling, tutor matching, communication, placement administration and follow-up.

Access

Operational records should be accessible only to authorised Tutor Match administrators. Do not submit passwords, payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive information through public forms.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Tutor Match through WhatsApp.
